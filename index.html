Income tax bands quiz

2026/27 rates for England, Wales and Northern Ireland. Income tax only; National Insurance is not included.

Rates and bands
  • Personal Allowance: £12,570 at 0%
  • Basic rate: next £37,700 of taxable income at 20%
  • Higher rate: taxable income above £37,700, up to £125,140 total income, at 40%
  • Additional rate: income over £125,140 at 45%
  • Allowance falls by £1 for every £2 of income over £100,000
